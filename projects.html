  • 2025 – 2026

    Context-Aware Speller

    A gaze speller that listens to the conversation partner and suggests letters, words and whole sentences.

    Read the summary

    A context-aware assistive speller designed for individuals with severe speech and motor impairments (e.g. ALS). Unlike traditional letter-by-letter spellers, it listens to the conversational partner, obtains user approval of the context, and then generates AI-powered suggestions at letter, word and full-sentence levels. The user retains full control and selects via gaze (or other pointing methods). Early evaluation on 50 real-life communication scenarios showed approximately 1.7× faster response times (≈40 % reduction) compared with a conventional eye-tracking speller operating at ~15 words per minute.

    Robot Car Movement Controlled by Eye-Tracking

    A mobile robot steered by the direction of the user’s gaze.

    Read the summary

    Developed a prototype system in which a mobile robot car is steered by eye-gaze direction. Eye-tracking data were mapped to motion commands, enabling hands-free directional control. The project combined basic gaze-estimation techniques with embedded motor control and served as an exploration of human–computer interaction for assistive scenarios.

  • 2021

    Obstacle-Avoiding Robot with Arduino

    An autonomous robot that avoids obstacles with ultrasonic sensing.

    Read the summary

    Designed and built an autonomous obstacle-avoiding mobile robot as part of IEEE YTU Student Branch activities. The system used ultrasonic sensors for distance measurement and an Arduino microcontroller for real-time control of differential-drive motors. Basic reactive navigation logic enabled the robot to detect and circumnavigate obstacles in unstructured indoor environments.
