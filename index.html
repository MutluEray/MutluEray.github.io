About

I am a biomedical engineer working across signal processing, machine learning and neurotechnology. My work spans wearable sensing, biosignal forecasting, transparent AI and embedded machine learning, and has been carried out at the Technical University of Denmark and Yıldız Technical University, together with industry partners.

Research

All research
  • 2024–now

    Driver drowsiness detection and forecasting

    Detecting drowsiness from wearable EEG during simulated driving, and forecasting future EEG states so a warning can come before vigilance drops.

    With the Technical University of Denmark and Cebreo Medical

  • 2025–now

    Physiology-inspired counterfactual explanations for ECG models

    Small, clinically meaningful edits to a heartbeat that flip a model’s prediction, showing which features drive it.

    With the Technical University of Denmark

  • 2022–2023

    A digital stethoscope with on-device machine learning

    An embedded network that recognises heart, lung and bowel sounds and filters each one, at a quarter of the original model size.

    With Yıldız Technical University, funded by TÜBİTAK

Selected projects

All 17 projects

Where to put a wearable EEG electrode

Tests whether a head model can choose where a two-electrode wearable should go, checked against real scalp, around-ear and in-ear EEG from 15 people.

Does left–right symmetry survive stroke?

Does a left–right symmetry prior still help motor-imagery decoding after stroke? In healthy subjects it carries real information; in patients it no longer helps.

Seizure onset as a bifurcation

Seizure onset modelled as a switch between two stable states in a 3,750-neuron excitatory/inhibitory network.

Do deep vision models predict human EEG?

Which layers of ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16 predict human EEG responses to images, and how much of that is low-level image structure?
