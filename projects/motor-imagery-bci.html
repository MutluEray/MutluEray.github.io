Motor Imagery BCI for Stroke Rehabilitation

Left- versus right-hand motor-imagery decoding for stroke rehabilitation with filter-bank CSP and Riemannian classifiers.

Code on GitHub
Year
2023
Type
g.tec BCI & Neurotechnology Spring School hackathon
Data
16-channel EEG, stroke patients

The goal was to decode left versus right hand motor imagery from 16-channel EEG for upper-extremity stroke rehabilitation. Implemented Filter Bank CSP and Riemannian-geometry classifiers (tangent-space logistic regression and MDM) alongside a regularized CSP+LDA baseline.

Our best model per session vs. both published benchmarks

Published benchmarks: Sebastián-Romagosa et al., Front. Neurosci. 2020 (CSP+LDA) and Gruenwald et al., Front. Neurosci. 2019 (TVLDA).

The classifier's actual feature (CSP log-variance) on held-out test trials — right- and left-hand imagery separate clearly once the model looks at the right spatial combination of channels, not any single electrode.
