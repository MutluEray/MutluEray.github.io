3D U-Net for Ischemic Stroke Lesion Segmentation

A patch-based 3D U-Net that segments ischemic stroke lesions from diffusion MRI.

Code on GitHub
Year
2024
Data
ISLES 2022 (250 public cases)
Status
Re-analysed and written up in 2026

A patch-based 3D U-Net that segments ischemic stroke lesions directly from diffusion-weighted MRI, trained on the ISLES 2022 dataset (250 cases, DWI + ADC). The network samples 3D patches biased toward lesion tissue during training, then reconstructs full-volume predictions at evaluation time via overlap-averaged sliding-window inference. All results are on a held-out split of the 250 public cases, not on the ISLES challenge test set, so they are not directly comparable with the challenge leaderboard.
