Self-tuning DBS under electrode drift

Each step, an algorithm picks a stimulation amplitude, observes a noisy score (beta suppression minus an energy cost), and adapts. Electrode drift changes how much current reaches tissue, so the best setting moves. Pick a drift scenario and two algorithms and watch them track it.

Runs entirely in your browser. The response curve is a surrogate fitted to 49 simulations of the Fleming, Dunn & Lowery (2020) cortico-basal ganglia model in NEURON (130 Hz, 2-s evaluations, noise measured over 3 burst patterns). Drift is modelled as a scaling of delivered amplitude. Algorithm settings are the fairly tuned values from the study; the time-varying GP uses a shorter memory (120 instead of 200 evaluations) to stay fast in the browser. One run is one random seed: the study reports means over 30 seeds.
